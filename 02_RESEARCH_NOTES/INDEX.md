# Alternative Research Directions

Index of non-transformer approaches to intelligence, per the project pivot
[[05_TEAM_DECISIONS/2026-04-28-abandon-transformer-paradigm|decision]].

## Core Paradigms

### Recurrent Architectures
- RNN variants (LSTM, GRU)
- Recurrent State Spaces (Mamba-style)
- Liquid Neural Networks
- Continuous-time RNNs

### Sparse Networks
- Sparse Mixture of Experts (non-transformer)
- Dynamic sparse training
- Lottery Ticket Hypothesis
- Sparse evolutionary training

### Modular/State-Based
- Neural module networks
- Compositional pattern generators
- State-space models
- Hierarchical state machines
- Graph neural networks

### Predictive Coding / Free Energy
- Predictive coding networks (Rao & Ballard)
- Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle
- Hierarchical predictive processing
- Active inference frameworks

### Evolutionary & Hebbian
- Neuroevolution (NEAT, HyperNEAT)
- Hebbian learning rules
- Spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP)
- Self-organizing maps
- Developmental/cognitive approaches

### World Models & Cognition
- Schmidhuber's world models
- Cognitive architectures (ACT-R, SOAR)
- Global Workspace Theory
- Integrated Information Theory (IIT)

## Open Questions

- How do these paradigms compose?
- Which combination yields general intelligence?
- What mathematical frameworks unify them?
- How do we evaluate intelligence independently of language tasks?
